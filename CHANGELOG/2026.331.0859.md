# 2026.331.0859 — docs(java-openjdk): drop the Android-consumer prose

## Summary

Drops the Android-consumer prose from the JDK layer. The `java-openjdk` candy stays live and unchanged in capability: it still installs OpenJDK 21, still symlinks `JAVA_HOME` to the distro-agnostic `/usr/lib/jvm/charly-jdk21`, and keeps every package arm, `run:` step and `check:` step.

This is the `layer-java-openjdk` leg of the approved Android/JetKVM retirement (opencharly/opencharly#1318, operator decision 2026-11-27, no replacement). Only sentences describing the layer as the Android SDK / Appium host or as serving the android-emulator are removed or rewritten; the non-Android consumers (Gradle, sbt) stay named.
